# GatesPort Aviation — Brand Quick Reference

## Name

Always write the company name as **GatesPort Aviation**, with Gates and Port joined as one word, matching the logo wordmark.

## Brands

GatesPort Aviation is the holding company. It has three subsidiaries, each with its own logo set in the same five colours:

- GatesPort Aviation — `gates-port-logo-*`
- GatesPort Cargo — `gates-port-cargo-logo-*`
- GatesPort Catering — `gates-port-catering-logo-*`
- GatesPort VIP — `gates-port-vip-logo-*`

All four share the GP emblem and the GATESPORT wordmark. Only the descriptor line changes (AVIATION, CARGO, CATERING, VIP).

## Colours

- Burgundy — `#48151D` — primary brand surfaces and actions
- Deep burgundy — `#2F0D13` — dense tonal depth
- Antique gold — `#B39567` — emblem and sparing accents
- Warm cream — `#F0E5D2` — primary light canvas and reversed text
- Bright cream — `#F7F0E4` — soft section contrast
- Espresso — `#241C19` — editorial dark surfaces

## Typography

- Display and headings: Montserrat, weights 500–700
- Body and interface copy: Source Sans 3, weights 400–700

Both type families are available through Google Fonts.

## Logo rules

- Prefer the full lockup whenever space allows.
- Use the emblem for compact applications where the name is already established.
- Keep clear space around the logo on every side.
- Use the full lockup at 140 px wide or larger on screen.
- Do not stretch, rotate, rearrange, outline or recolour the logo outside the approved palette.
- Do not place the logo over busy or low-contrast imagery.

The supplied files are transparent PNG assets derived from the approved source artwork.
