Consistency protects recognition. Keep the mark clear, confident and free from visual interference.

Clear space

Minimum size

Use the full lockup at 140 pixels wide or larger on screen. Below that, use the emblem.

Keep the signature intact

  • Do not stretch, compress or rotate the logo.

  • Do not recolour outside the approved palette.

  • Do not place the logo over busy or low-contrast imagery.

  • Do not rearrange the emblem, name or descriptor.
