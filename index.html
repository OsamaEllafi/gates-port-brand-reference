07

Logo usage

Give it room to travel

Consistency protects recognition. Keep the mark clear, confident and free from visual interference.

1× 1×
01 / Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the height of the “G” counter on every side.

140 px minimum
02 / Minimum size

Use the full lockup at 140 pixels wide or larger on screen. Below that, use the emblem.

Non-negotiables

Keep the signature intact.

  • 01Do not stretch, compress or rotate the logo.
  • 02Do not recolour outside the approved palette.
  • 03Do not place the logo over busy or low-contrast imagery.
  • 04Do not rearrange the emblem, name or descriptor.
