# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing: a static HTML, CSS and JavaScript site (no build step), published on GitHub Pages from the `main` branch of `OsamaEllafi/gates-port-brand-reference`. Logo files are generated by `tools/build_brand_assets.py` (Python, Pillow, OpenCV).

## Users

Three audiences, all confirmed:

- Staff and management of the GatesPort group, who need the right logo or colour quickly and want to see how the four brands relate.
- Agencies, designers, sign-makers, printers and web developers outside the company, who must apply the identity correctly.
- Partners, airline customers and press, who need an approved logo to publish.

## Product Purpose

The official brand reference for GatesPort Aviation and its three subsidiaries. A visitor should be able, within seconds, to grab a logo file, understand that the brands form a family, and learn the usage rules. Success is the correct logo in the correct colour used correctly, with no email to ask for it.

## Positioning

The single authoritative source for the GatesPort identity: one holding brand and three subsidiaries that share one emblem and one wordmark, differing only in the descriptor line.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a shared link, often on a phone or a work laptop, to complete a small task: copy a logo into a document or presentation, download a PNG for print or web, or check a hex code. The brand is applied in the real world on ramp vehicles, crew badges, menus, napkins and the website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four brands: GatesPort Aviation (holding company), GatesPort Cargo, GatesPort Catering, GatesPort VIP.
- Each brand has five single-colour logos (burgundy, antique gold, warm cream, black, white) as PNG and SVG, generated by `tools/build_brand_assets.py`. The GP emblem is shared and also ships separately.
- Existing functions that must keep working: copy a logo to the clipboard, download a logo PNG, copy a colour HEX, download the complete logo pack (`assets/gates-port-brand-assets.zip`).
- Existing guidance to preserve: clear space, minimum size (140 px wide), four logo don'ts, Montserrat and Source Sans 3 typography, the colour palette.
- Written company name: **GatesPort Aviation** (one word, "GatesPort"). The wordmark is GATESPORT over a descriptor (AVIATION, CARGO, CATERING, VIP).
- English now; the structure must be able to take an Arabic (right-to-left) version later without a rebuild. Do not write Arabic company copy.
- Deployed as static files on GitHub Pages. No server-side code.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the GP emblem and the GATESPORT lockups exactly as supplied. Do not redraw, restyle or recolour the logos.
- Palette: Burgundy `#48151D`, Deep burgundy `#2F0D13`, Antique gold `#B39567`, Warm cream `#F0E5D2`, Bright cream `#F7F0E4`, Espresso `#241C19`.
- Typefaces: Montserrat for headings, Source Sans 3 for body copy.
- Voice: composed, clear, capable, attentive. Tagline: "Every journey, handled with care." Boards also use "People further" and "Benghazi / Aviation / Travel".
- Location: Benghazi, Libya.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo files for all four brands in `assets/logos/`.
- Approved identity board `../brand-identity-concepts/07-signature-service.png` (primary logo on burgundy, palette, typography, ramp-vehicle and napkin applications) and approved hero concept `../hero-concepts/gates-port-aviation-hero.png` (burgundy sculpted frames with gold edge lines echoing the emblem, apron scene).
- Company profile `../Company Profile.md` and aviation photographs in `../assets/images/` with credits in `IMAGE-CREDITS.md`.
- No testimonials, verified metrics, certifications or contact details exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Task first: the right file in the right colour in two interactions.
2. Show the family: Aviation as holding company with Cargo, Catering and VIP, read at a glance.
3. Teach by showing the logo in use, not only by listing rules.
4. The identity guards itself: the page may never distort, recolour or crowd the logos it presents.
5. Built to carry a right-to-left version later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable, visible focus, sufficient contrast on every logo-on-background pairing, respects reduced motion. Layout must tolerate right-to-left mirroring later.
